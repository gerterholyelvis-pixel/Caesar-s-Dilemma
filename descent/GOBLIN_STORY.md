# GRIK, the first statue

## Who he was
Grik kept the graveyard under the hill, a mortal with a shovel, a lantern and a ledger. He buried the dead and, as gravediggers did, kept their coins: one for the ferryman he never believed in, the rest for himself. He counted nightly, always eight, always the same eight, because counting was the only prayer he had. He was not cruel. He was tallying. He wanted, more than anything, for the sum to mean something.

## The bargain
A small black angel found him at the grave's edge. Faceless, wingspan of a child, and on its head a red hat, the only colour in the cemetery. It came from Limbo, the place where unfinished sums go. It did not threaten. It offered *more*: more coins, more nights, a count that never ended. It held out a single coin that had no face on it either.
Grik took it. The price was never said aloud because he never asked, and that is the price: whoever takes the coin is agreeing not to ask.
He fell. Not down: *through*. Skin to glass, glass to obsidian. The ears sharpened first. The coins streamed upward out of his pockets, toward a small red dot, the hat, shrinking.

## Why first of five
The angel does not bargain once. It keeps a set: five statues, five who said yes, and each carved by what they wanted. Grik was first because he wanted the least, a number, so he was the easiest to keep. His stone is the proof the rest can be done. (Lines, never explained:)
- The devil: *he asked for a name that would be feared; he was given one nobody remembers.*
- The angel: *she was already holy; it only asked her to stop looking down.*
- The horror: *there are too many eyes, and every one of them is his.*
- The plain dark man: *he asked for nothing. Look closer. That was the price too.*

## What Grik knows
- The coin has a glyph and the glyph has a glyph: the same broken triangle, folded smaller inside itself, forever. That is what "more" looks like from inside.
- Glass remembers every lie; stone only waits. He is both now.
- The hat is not a hat. It is a point. Everything he fell toward is that red point.
- He is still counting. When the eyes open he is not waking; he is *noticing who is counting him back.*

## Motifs
Red hat = the only red; always first and last. Fractal glyph = the Sierpinski triangle etched in gold: on the title, on the mirror, on the coin. Eight coins. Five sigils (goblin, horns, halo, ring of eyes, a plain circle). Ember vs. cold glass. Silence is the angel's voice.
