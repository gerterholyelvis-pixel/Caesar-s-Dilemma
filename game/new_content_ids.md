# New content for art (ids -> visual ideas)

- mirror_shard: crystal mirror, 1 cell. Cracked silver-teal sliver, faint reflected eye.
- ember_censer: fire tool censer, 1x2. Hanging bronze bowl, violet-gold smoke writing glyphs.

- tide_flute: water instrument bone, 1x3 line. Pale bone flute, teal ripples spiralling off the holes.
- oath_stone: tool stone, passive, 1x2. Dark standing stone, one gold rune seam.
- cursed_crown: cursed crown weapon, arch (5 cells). Thorned black crown, violet-red-free glow, gold rim, dripping shadow.
- hungry_maw: living bone cursed, C-shape (5 cells). Toothed jaw, bone teeth, violet gullet.
- echo_bell: echo crystal bell, 3 cells bent. Glass bell with trailing ghost rings, cyan.
- spiral_shell: fractal water armor, S (4 cells). Nautilus whorl, each turn a smaller copy, teal-gold.
- hollow_idol: charm idol cursed, passive, 1x2. Empty-eyed gold figurine, dark hollow chest.
- grafted_heart: living cursed heart, vertical S (4 cells). Stitched violet heart with plant roots, faint pulse.
